/// THE FOUNDER

THE MINDS BEHIND THE MACHINES

KRS KING (Pvt) Ltd runs three focused divisions — Falcon ICM for innovative construction & management, Panther EC for engineering construction, and Phoenix IA for innovative agriculture. What began at a lathe in a backyard shed is now an engineering company where farmers, builders and businesses bring impossible briefs.

The rule here is simple: if a customer needs it and it doesn't exist — we invent it. Every design is researched, prototyped and tested in-house, then backed with training, spare parts and support. Construction and civil projects get the same engineering care, end to end.
